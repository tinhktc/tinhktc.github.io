Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Drag across empty tiles to create a region that matches one of the target shapes. Fill the whole board to complete the level.

What do red walls mean?

Red walls block a region. A shape cannot pass through a wall, so you need to plan each region around those barriers.

How do hints work?

Hints highlight a valid next region when you get stuck. New players receive starter hints, and the game also provides a daily hint reward.

My progress looks wrong. What should I do?

Please send us your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are also helpful when reporting an issue.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Tile Region is designed to be playable offline.

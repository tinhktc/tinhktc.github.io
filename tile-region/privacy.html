Privacy Policy

Tile Region: Brain Puzzle

This Privacy Policy explains how Tile Region: Brain Puzzle ("the App") handles user information.

Information We Collect

The App does not require account creation and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Gameplay Data

The App stores limited gameplay progress on your device, such as your current level, completed levels, and hint balance, so the game can work properly for you.

This data is stored locally on your device and is not sold to third parties.

Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not use your data for cross-app tracking.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We do not knowingly share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed as a puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We aim to keep the App simple and privacy-friendly. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

Because gameplay data is stored locally on your device, you can remove the App or clear its data from your device to reset local game progress.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with the revised effective date.
